# Otter Spa Tycoon account deletion

[한국어](../delete-account) · [日本語](../ja/delete-account) · [中文](../zh/delete-account) · [Español](../es/delete-account)

Developer: Minyoung Kim

## Delete in the game

1. Open the game and tap **Settings** at the top right.
2. Tap **Cloud save → Delete account** and confirm.

Your account ID, email address and the game progress backed up to the cloud are deleted immediately and can't be recovered. Progress stored on your device stays until you uninstall the app.

## If you can't use the app

Email omeano.kim@gmail.com with the subject "Otter Spa Tycoon account deletion request" and the email address you signed in with (Google or Apple). We will verify the request and delete your data within 30 days, then let you know.

## Data kept after deletion

- Purchase verification records (transaction IDs): kept for up to 5 years so the same purchase is never delivered twice, then deleted.
- Purchase history itself is kept by Apple App Store and Google Play.

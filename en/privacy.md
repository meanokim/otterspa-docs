# Otter Spa Tycoon Privacy Policy

[한국어](../privacy) · [日本語](../ja/privacy) · [中文](../zh/privacy) · [Español](../es/privacy)

Minyoung Kim ("the developer") provides the mobile game "Otter Spa Tycoon" ("the game") and cares about your privacy. This policy explains what information the game processes, why, and what rights you have.

## 1. Accounts and cloud save

The game has no separate sign-up and never asks for your name or phone number.

- **Cloud backup**: so you can continue on a new device, your game progress (facilities, currencies, staff, missions, tutorial step, etc.) is backed up to Google Firebase servers. If you don't sign in, it is backed up under a randomly created anonymous account ID.
- **Sign in with Google or Apple (optional)**: if you sign in from Settings, we receive that account's unique ID and email address (a relay address if you use Apple's "Hide My Email") and link your backup to it. We never receive your password.
- You can play the game without backup or sign-in.

## 2. Information processed automatically

| Information | Purpose | Processed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advertising ID (IDFA / Android advertising ID), device info (model, OS version, language), IP address, ad interactions | Showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks | Google AdMob |
| App usage (game events such as tutorial progress, building and upgrading facilities, opening branches, claiming ad rewards), app instance ID, device info | Statistics to improve and balance the game | Google Firebase Analytics |
| App instance ID, device info | Delivering game settings (balance multipliers, event schedules) | Google Firebase Remote Config |
| Crash reports (error details, device and OS info) | Fixing bugs and improving stability | Google Firebase Crashlytics |
| IP address (time check request) | Checking the current time on Google's servers so idle rewards cannot be inflated by changing the device clock | Google |
| Purchase history (product, transaction ID) | Delivering and restoring paid items | Apple App Store, Google Play |
| Account ID (anonymous, Google or Apple), email address (if signed in), game progress, a random device ID | Cloud backup and loading on another device | Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Firestore) |
| Purchase verification data (product, transaction ID, purchase token) | Checking with the store that a purchase is real to prevent fraudulent delivery | Google Firebase Cloud Functions, Apple App Store, Google Play |

- Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by each store; the developer never receives them.
- On iOS, the game asks for App Tracking Transparency permission first and does not use the advertising ID if you decline.
- In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and similar regions, ad privacy consent is collected through Google's consent management platform (UMP). You can change it anytime in **Settings → Ad privacy settings** in the game.

## 3. Retention

- Game data on your device: deleted when you uninstall the app.
- Cloud backup and account information: kept until you delete your account, then deleted immediately. Backups of anonymous accounts unused for 2 years are also deleted.
- Purchase verification records (transaction IDs): kept for up to 5 years, even after account deletion, so the same purchase is never delivered twice.
- Firebase Analytics data: kept for up to 14 months, then deleted.
- Crashlytics data: kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.
- Ad and purchase information: subject to each provider's (Google, Apple) policies.

## 4. Sharing and processors

The developer does not sell your information. It is processed only by the service providers in section 2, for the purposes listed there. These providers may store data outside your country (for example, in the United States).

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 5. Your rights

- Opt out of ad tracking: iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**; Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads**.
- Change ad privacy consent: **Settings → Ad privacy settings** in the game (shown only in regions where it applies).
- Delete game data: uninstalling the app deletes all game data on the device.
- Delete your account and cloud backup: **Settings → Cloud save → Delete account** in the game deletes them right away. If you can't use the app, see [Account deletion](delete-account) to request it by email.
- For any other access, correction, or deletion request, please contact us below.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 14 (or the minimum age in your country), and the developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

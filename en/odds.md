# Otter Spa Tycoon Drop Rates

[한국어](../odds) · [日本語](../ja/odds) · [中文](../zh/odds) · [Español](../es/odds)

These are the odds for every item or reward in the game whose result is random. If the odds change, this page is updated first.

## 1. Staff Recruit Chest

How to get: buy with 60 gems (Shop), watch a rewarded ad (3 per day), branch progress rewards, mission rewards

When a chest is opened, the result type is decided first.

| Result | Odds (you have at least 1 staff) | Odds (no staff yet) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 150 training EXP | 50% | 0% |
| 1 staff member | 50% | 100% |

If a staff member is drawn, a rarity is chosen, then one of the 5 staff of that rarity with equal odds.

| Rarity | Rarity odds | Odds per staff | Staff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 75% | 15% | Apprentice Scrubber, Snack Bar Helper, Rookie Cleaner, Rookie Stoker, Rookie Clerk |
| Rare | 22% | 4.4% | Veteran Scrubber, Snack Chef, Tidy Cleaner, Trusty Stoker, Smiley Clerk |
| Legendary | 3% | 0.6% | Legendary Scrub Master, Baked Egg Master, Sparkle Cleaning King, Flame Boiler Master, Veteran Manager |

- Overall odds when you have at least 1 staff: training EXP 50%, each common staff 7.5%, each rare staff 2.2%, each legendary staff 0.3%.
- A staff member you already own turns into EXP for that staff (common 60, rare 150, legendary 400).

## 2. Lucky Towel Chest

How to get: watch a rewarded ad (5 per day). Not sold for money.

| Result | Odds | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gems | 30% | 2–10 gems (each amount equally likely, about 3.33% each) |
| Coins | 70% | 5–20 minutes of your current income per minute (uniform) |

## 3. Fixed-content products (not random)

- Starter Pack: 300 gems + 1 rare staff (chosen with equal odds among rare staff you don't own yet; 150 EXP if you own them all) + Sheep Towel costume (for otter family guests)
